About SPN Alphabetz International School

We nurture young minds with quality education, strong values and holistic development.From academic to co-curricular activities , we ensure every child grows with confidence and competence.

Our curriculum resonates and compromises both Indian culture and montessari approach for our childern to make them self sufficient and well equipped for the next step in their educational journey. We welcome you to step into a world where learning meets excellence at SPN Alphabetz International School. Join us in shaping a brighter future for your child, where every day is an opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive.

Facilities available :

  • Spacious classrooms
  • Scholastic lab
  • Story lab
  • Library
  • Indoor and outdoor play areas
  • Qualified teachers
  • Experienced caretakers
  • CCTV surveillance
  • Transportation facilities

Our Vision

Our vision is to ignite a lifelong love for learning and nurture confident, independent learners who are ready to face future challenges with courage and resilience.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, secure, and positive learning environment where young learners build a strong foundation through creative, engaging, and activity-based teaching methods.
